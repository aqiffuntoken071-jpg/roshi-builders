# Roshi Builders Digital

Build a long, modern, visually attractive multi-page website for a UK-based construction and home improvement company called Roshi Builders. Use a clean, professional, premium construction-industry aesthetic — dark red/maroon (#8B2332 or similar) and white/charcoal color scheme to match the brand logo (a red rooftop/house icon with a hammer). Use bold modern sans-serif fonts, generous whitespace, smooth scroll animations, hover effects on cards/buttons, and a sticky navigation bar.

The website must be fully responsive and work perfectly on every device — mobile phones (small and large screens), tablets (portrait and landscape), laptops, and large desktop monitors. Use a mobile-first responsive design approach with fluid grids, flexible images, and breakpoints for all common screen sizes (320px, 375px, 480px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px, 1440px, and above). Navigation should collapse into a clean hamburger menu on mobile/tablet. All buttons, forms, galleries, and text must remain fully usable and legible without horizontal scrolling or overlapping elements on any device. Test layout consistency across iOS, Android, and desktop browsers.

Pages required (7 total):

Home – Hero section with a strong headline (e.g. "Quality Craftsmanship You Can Trust"), background image/slideshow of finished projects, a short intro about Roshi Builders, a "Get a Free Quote" CTA button, a services preview grid (icons + short descriptions), a featured project gallery preview, customer trust badges/highlights, and a call-to-action footer banner with the phone number.

About Us – Company story/mission, years of experience, why choose us (quality, reliability, attention to detail), team/owner intro (Roshi), values section, and a small trust-building stats bar (projects completed, happy clients, etc.).

Services – Detailed service listing page with individual sections/cards for each service, using icons and descriptions:

Tiling

Painting and Decorating

Laminate Flooring

Loft Conversions

Plastering

Gardening Work

Media Wall Installation

Gallery / Portfolio – A masonry/grid photo gallery showcasing completed work (ceiling lighting installs, media walls with fireplaces and mounted TVs, tiled flooring, plastering, painting, bathrooms). Include filter tabs by service category and lightbox image viewing, with a responsive grid that adjusts columns per screen size (1 column mobile, 2 columns tablet, 3–4 columns desktop).

Testimonials – Customer review cards/carousel with star ratings, names, and short quotes about quality of work and reliability. Carousel must be swipeable on touch devices.

FAQ – Common questions (areas covered, free quotes, timelines, materials, guarantees, payment) in an expandable accordion format that works smoothly with touch and click.

Contact Us – A clean contact page with:

A simple contact form (Name, Email, Phone, Service Needed, Message), stacked full-width on mobile

Click-to-call phone number: 07918 208882

Social media icons linking to Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok

A map/service area section

A final CTA: "Contact Roshi today for a free quote"

Additional requirements:

Consistent header/footer across all pages with logo, navigation menu, and phone number always visible in the top nav (phone number tappable to call on mobile)

Footer with quick links, services list, social icons, and contact number, stacked cleanly on smaller screens

Smooth page transitions and modern micro-interactions

Fast-loading, SEO-friendly structure with proper headings

Images optimized and responsive (srcset/auto-scaling) so they load fast and look sharp on all screen sizes
